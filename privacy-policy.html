Privacy policy

What we collect

When you send a quote request or booking enquiry, we collect the details you provide — your name, contact information, event date, location and the rentals you are interested in. Our online booking is handled through Booqable, which processes the dates, items and payment details you enter there.

How we use it

We use your details only to prepare your quote, confirm availability and pricing, coordinate delivery and setup, and stay in touch about your event. We do not sell your information.

Sharing

We share information only where it is needed to run your booking — for example with our booking provider, Booqable — or where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions about your information? Email dhillontents@gmail.com or call +1 (416) 893-2626 and we will help.

Your planning details in this browser

Your browser can temporarily retain your quote list and unsent form details for this tab so they remain available as you move between pages. Your booking cart and checkout are handled by Booqable.
