01

Make a smaller footprint work harder

For a city backyard or compact event space, a tent’s width can matter as much as its capacity. Measure the clear setup area and leave room for guests to enter and move between tables. Use the tent planner with your yard dimensions before choosing a size.

02

Plan the route from the truck to the setup

Narrow side passages, steps, laneways and shared driveways can affect installation access. Send a few photos showing the full route and tell us about any loading or parking arrangements at the venue. We review access before confirming a setup.

03

Bring your venue details into the plan

For a Toronto venue or organised outdoor event, share the site contact, delivery windows, surface type and any venue requirements already provided to you. Tent selection and anchoring need to suit that site. Delivery availability and all quoted charges are confirmed before you reserve.
