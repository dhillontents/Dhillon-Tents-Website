A little planning goes a long way

Your tent rental, step by step.

01

Start with your guests and space

Measure the usable width and length, then choose standing, seated dining or a full reception in our tent size planner. Leave room for entrances, catering and any dance floor. We review the final fit before installation.

02

Bring the rental list together

Combine your tent with chairs and tables, string lighting or a dance floor. For a ceremony and reception, our wedding tent rental guide helps you think through the whole day.

03

Confirm the delivery plan

Send the address, date, surface type and a few site photos. Include gates, steps, venue loading windows and the path from the vehicle to the tent. Your quote confirms equipment availability, delivery, installation, pickup and pricing.
