A tent that works with your property

The right size depends on both the guest count and the usable space. Start with 10 sq ft per seated guest or 60 standing guests per 400 sq ft, then share the width and length of your setup area, the surface type and a few photos. Gates, overhead obstructions, slopes and installation access can all change the plan.

Brampton event rentals, brought together

A birthday may need covered dining and a buffet table. A wedding weekend might also need a dance floor, lighting and room for vendors. Combine your Brampton tent rental with chairs and tables so each part of the setup works together.

Delivery, setup and takedown in Brampton
